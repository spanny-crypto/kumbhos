KumbhOS helps pilgrims stay safe and find their way around the Nashik Kumbh Mela — live infrastructure maps, ID wristbands for kids & elders, one-tap emergency SOS, a trip planner built on real ghat and hospital locations, and a heritage guide to the city.

🤖 Download for Android Direct APK · works offline 🍎 Download for iOS Coming soon

Android app is a direct APK install (enable "install from unknown sources" if prompted). iOS packaging needs a Mac build machine and isn't available yet — the web app works great on iPhone in the meantime.

What's inside

Everything built for the scale of a Kumbh gathering — and honest about what's real data vs. a prototype.

🗺️

Nashik Infrastructure Map

Real surveyed ghats, hospitals, fire stations, toilets, and parking across Nashik–Trimbakeshwar.

🧭

Trip Planner

Find the nearest real facility to any landmark or your live location — not a synthetic demo.

🆔

ID Wristbands

Printable QR wristbands for children & elders — scan links straight to a guardian's phone number, no app needed.

🚨

Emergency SOS

One-tap real emergency dial (police/ambulance) plus live location sharing.

🛕

Nashik Heritage Guide

History and context on Trimbakeshwar, Ramkund, Panchavati, and more — for visitors who've never been.

💰

Fair Price Guide

Typical local prices for food, transport, and essentials so out-of-town visitors don't get overcharged.

🔍

Lost & Found

Report and search for missing persons and items in one place.

📡

Live Billboard

A simple, high-contrast running feed of the most important alerts, for anyone at a glance.
